# {{TITLE}} style: build rules

Every number here comes from `style-guide.md` (measured on the reference reels) and is checked by
`references/style-spec.json` (the match scorer). Write rules a builder can follow without looking at
the references. Delete any section the style doesn't use.

## Canvas and pacing
- 1080x1920, 30 fps. Cuts per minute, average shot, visual changes per second: (from the spec).

## Layouts
For each layout: what fills the frame, where the face sits (x/y/size), share of runtime, when it's used
(which kind of line), how it starts and ends (cut / move).

## Captions
Font (the font-match winner, weight, tracking), size in px, colour, position per layout (y px), words
per chunk, case and punctuation, highlight or emphasis rule, animation in/out.

## Titles and on-screen text
Position, size, font mix, where the claim goes, how text enters.

## Graphic building blocks
Each recurring element: look (exact colours, radius, size), how it enters, how long it stays, what
spoken moment triggers it. Link each to an example scene in `template/examples/`.

## Colour
Background(s), text, accent(s) and the share of colour on screen (the spec's red_share or equivalent).

## Motion
Entrances, camera moves, exits, easing, stagger.

## Zooms, B-roll, transitions
What exists and when; what never happens.

## Sound
Music yes/no and level, sound effects per animation type (see `sound-map.md`), loudness target.
Record the ear-check answers here.

## Opening and ending
First 3 seconds; the CTA end.
