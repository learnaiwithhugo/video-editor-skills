# How to film for this edit (give this to the person filming)

The edit can only be as clean as the footage. Five minutes of setup saves an hour of fixing.

## The shot
- **One camera, locked off** (tripod or a stack of books), at eye level. Don't move it between takes.
- **Best: horizontal 4K (3840x2160), a WIDE shot**: head, shoulders and some room, you in the
  centre. The face card shows the whole frame small, and the full-screen face shot is a ~3x crop
  of it, so 4K keeps your face sharp.
- **Phone vertical works too** (what most people have): stand or sit further back than feels
  natural, so there's space above your head and your chest is in frame. Too close and the card
  can only show your face from forehead to chin.
- Keep your head roughly in the same spot: the editor frames the card around where your head sits.

## Light and background
- **A dark room with a warm light on your face** (a lamp or a window in front of you, never
  behind). One or two coloured lights behind you (an LED strip) look great.
- **Wear a dark top.** The editor cuts your head out so it pops over the edge of the card; a dark
  top and hair against a plain-ish background give the cleanest cut.
- Avoid a bright window or white wall directly behind your head (it leaves a light rim on your hair).

## Talking
- Talk straight through. Fluffed a line? Pause a second and say the WHOLE line again. The editor
  finds the last clean take of every line and cuts all the pauses out.
- Say each line as one breath, in the order of the script. Hook first.
- Say product names clearly ("Claude", "Opus 5.5"): speech-to-text mishears them.
- Hold the camera mic or a lav close; a quiet room matters more than an expensive mic.

## Then
Copy the clips into the project's `raw/` folder (copies, keep the originals) and run `/kallaway-edit`.
