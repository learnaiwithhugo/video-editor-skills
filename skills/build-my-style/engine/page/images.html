Image rules

Defaults
  1. No rounded corners on images. Sharp rectangles, with no glassy bubble or tile around them.
  2. No stock photos. Nothing posed, studio-lit or corporate.
  3. Nothing that looks AI-made. No plastic skin, no too-perfect render look.
  4. No zooming on images. A picture lands on screen, sits still, then hard cuts. Zoom only when there is a reason.
  5. Real, candid, good-looking photos with real light. Film and pop-culture stills work for jokes. No burned-in text, watermarks, magazine covers or app screenshots.
