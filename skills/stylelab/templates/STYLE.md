## How to use this guide (read first)

**This is a set of guardrails, not a menu.** It fixes the LOOK: colours, font, how things move, how loud
sounds are, where text is safe, and what's banned. It does NOT limit what you make. For every line of a
script, ask what visual would explain THIS idea best, then design it fresh inside these rules. The kept
options below are examples of the look, not a list to pick from. Reuse one only when it's genuinely the
best fit, and mix things up across a video.

When the person gives a note on an edit ("I don't like X"), write it in here as a dated rule or into
Rejected, so the next edit already knows.

**Only finished decisions live here.** Everything they say along the way is kept word for word in
`notes/their-words.md`. A section here gets written once they've said it's how they want it, in their
words, with the exact numbers.

## The only fixed rules

- Text stays inside the platform's safe zone (below).
- Logos and screenshots are real: icons from the actual apps, screenshots taken once the page has fully loaded.
- Copy what reference videos DO (timing, motion, layout), never what they literally show, unless the person
  asks for a part to be copied exactly (then rebuild it near-exactly, in their brand only).

Everything else comes from their brief and their rounds.

## Safe zone

Instagram organic Reels on a 1080x1920 frame: keep text out of the top 220 px and the bottom 420 px, and
out of the right-hand button column (x over 940, between y 1000 and 1500). The page's Safe zones button
draws this over every option. For other shapes (16:9, square) write the safe area here once the brief says.
